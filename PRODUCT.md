# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are hiring managers reviewing candidates for pet-product TikTok and Instagram Reels work. They need to assess creative range, practical production capability, and fit quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

This is a one-page short-video portfolio for HIROKO SASAKI. Its purpose is to make supplied public work easy to view and to communicate the supported production capabilities without inventing client details or outcomes. Success is a recruiter understanding the offering within one to two minutes.

## Positioning

Short-form video work is presented as a considered production practice: planning, filming, editing, and publishing are organised around the message and the audience.

## Conversion & proof

The primary next step is an enquiry about short-video or SNS content production. The fallback is reviewing the supplied video work and existing Instagram links. The proof on hand is the six user-approved original video files in materials/ and the four links in materials/references/公開URL一覧.txt.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and observant. The experience should feel clean and contemporary, with the video work carrying the personality rather than decorative pet imagery. It should convey confident care for product, audience, and animal-related content.

## Anti-references

Avoid a cute pet-shop aesthetic, dense template cards, flashy gradients, device mockups, invented social proof, and any claim not supported by supplied materials.

## Design Principles

- Let the actual videos provide the visual evidence.
- Make the first minute of review decisive and low-friction.
- Use calm hierarchy and whitespace to communicate professional judgement.
- Preserve source truth: unknown responsibilities remain explicitly unverified.
- Design for controlled, readable viewing on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site supports responsive reflow, native video controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. It does not rely on autoplay, sound, or motion to convey essential information.
